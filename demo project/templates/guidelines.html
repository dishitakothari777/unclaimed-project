Community Guidelines & Terms of Care

Principles that make The Unclaimed Project a warm, honest, and creative sanctuary for unfinished things.

Our Maker Code

1. No Creative Shame

Leaving a project unfinished is never a failure; it is simply proof that you were brave enough to try something new. We speak of paused projects with respect, not guilt.

2. Transparent Backstories

Be candid about why you stopped, what flaws or mistakes might be in the piece, and what materials are actually included. Rescuers appreciate knowing the truth of where things stand.

3. Respectful Adoption & Giving Credit

When adopting someone else’s unfinished piece, honor the time they put into it. Where appropriate, credit the original maker when sharing the finished result with the community.

4. Free from Commercial Speculation

The Unclaimed Project is built on gift culture, mutual support, and non-commercial creative exchange. Do not claim community-gifted materials to immediately flip them for profit elsewhere.
